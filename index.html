Travel Tips

Useful Tips for a Better Journey

Plan wisely and travel confidently.

Packing Tips

Pack light clothes for summer, layered outfits for winter, power bank, ID proof, medicines, and comfortable footwear.

Safety Tips

Keep digital and physical copies of documents, avoid isolated places late at night, and use verified transport options.

Emergency Numbers

National Emergency: 112, Police: 100, Ambulance: 108, Fire: 101.

Local Customs

Respect dress codes in religious places, remove shoes where required, and be mindful of local traditions and photography rules.

Best Travel Seasons

October to March is generally ideal for many destinations in India due to pleasant weather and festival seasons.

Planning Tips

Book hotels early during holidays, check local weather, and create a basic itinerary for smooth travel.
